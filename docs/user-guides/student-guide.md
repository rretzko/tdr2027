# StudentFolder.info — Student Guide

## Welcome

StudentFolder.info is where you manage your honors choir auditions online — from joining your school and teacher, to registering for an Event, to uploading an audition recording and paying any fees. Everything here connects back to your music teacher's own account on TheDirectorsRoom.com, so whatever you enter shows up for them too, and whatever they enter (or have already entered for you) shows up here.

You'll use this same account year after year, for every Event your teacher enrolls you in — there's no need to re-enter your information each time a new audition season opens.

## Getting started

Go to **studentfolder.info** and choose **Register** to create your own account (or **Log In** if you already have one). Registration asks for your name, pronouns, a password, and — optionally — an email address and cell phone number. Your password needs at least eight characters, with upper- and lowercase letters and a number. If you don't have your own email address, you can leave it blank and the system will set one up for you automatically. You'll need that address to log in, so find it on your **Profile** page and write it down before you log out. Also know that without a real email address, you won't be able to use "Forgot password" to reset your own login later, and you'll need to ask your teacher to reset it for you instead.

If your email address looks like a school-issued address (for example, ending in your district's domain), you may see an advisory when you try to reset your password, warning that school email systems often block password-reset emails. If that happens and you don't receive the reset email, ask your teacher — teachers can reset a student's password from their own account.

If you registered with your own email address, you may be asked to verify it first: open the message we send you and select the link. Many school email systems block that message, so addresses we recognize as school-issued skip this step. If yours is asked to verify and nothing arrives, check your spam folder, select **Resend verification email**, or ask your teacher, who can add you to their roster in the meantime.

The first time you get in, you'll land on the **School** page. This is a required first step — you can't reach your Events, or do much of anything else useful, until you've joined a school and at least one teacher there. After that, you'll land on your **Dashboard**, which lists your open Events.

The sidebar takes you everywhere else. Besides the pages described below, it has **Profile** (your name, pronouns, email, cell phone, and photo), **Password**, **Feedback** (report a bug or suggest an improvement), **User Guides** (this guide), and a light/dark switch. The Dashboard, School, and Event pages each have a **Take a tour** button.

## Walkthrough

### 1. School — join your school and teacher(s)

This is the page that unlocks everything else. Here's how it works:

1. Pick your **state** (defaults to New Jersey) and, optionally, a **zip code** to narrow the search.
2. Start typing your **school name**. Matching schools appear as you type — select **"This is my school."**
3. Choose your **grade**.
4. Under **your teacher(s) at this school**, check the box next to each teacher you work with, and for each one, check the **subject(s)** you have with them (e.g., Chorus, General Music). You must select at least one teacher and at least one subject before you can continue.
5. Select **Join School**.

A few things to know:

- **You can only join a school and teacher that are already in the system.** There's no "my school isn't listed" option for students — if your school or teacher doesn't show up, ask your teacher to finish setting up their own account on TheDirectorsRoom.com first (schools and teachers are added on the teacher side, not the student side).
- If you choose your school and no teachers appear, it usually means your teacher hasn't finished verifying their school email yet — check back later or ask them directly.
- **If you're told someone with your name is already enrolled at this school**, and that's you, log out and log back in with your original account, or ask your teacher for help. Don't join again with a second account. If it really is someone else, select **That's a different person**, then **Join School**.
- You can only be actively enrolled at **one school at a time**. If you switch schools later, come back to this page and join the new one — your prior school is deactivated automatically.
- Joining a teacher is what makes everything else happen: the moment you're linked to a teacher, you're automatically entered as a Candidate in any active Event they're already participating in that accepts your grade. You don't need to "sign up" for those separately — they'll simply appear on your **My Events** page.

### 2. Student Details — your profile

Found under **Student Details** in the sidebar. This page covers:

- **Preferred voice part** (e.g., Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass)
- **Shirt size** — this field is required; you can't save this page without picking one
- **Birthday**
- **Height**, chosen from a dropdown (shown in inches with the feet/inches equivalent, e.g., "74 (6'2")")
- **Home address** — street address, city, state, and zip. This only saves once all four of those fields are filled in; a partially filled address won't be stored.

Your grade is shown at the top of this page for reference, but it's set when you join your school, not edited here.

Select **Save** when you're done. You can come back and update these details any time.

### 3. Emergency Contacts

Found under **Emergency Contacts** in the sidebar. Add one or more people your teacher/school can reach in an emergency:

- Select **Add** to open the form.
- Fill in **name** and **relationship**, then add every way to reach them that you can: cell, work, or home phone, and email. Some Events require a cell phone, an email, or both, so it's best to include them.
- Use **Edit** or **Remove** on any existing contact card to update or delete it.

You can have as many emergency contacts as you need — most Events only require at least one to be on file.

### 4. My Events

Found under **My Events** in the sidebar (this page — and the individual Event pages under it — only becomes available once you've joined a school and teacher). This is a simple list of the open Events you've been entered into. An Event leaves the list once its organizer closes it. Each row shows:

- The **Event Version** name and its parent Event
- The **registration period** — when it opens, when it closes, or "Registration closed"
- Your current **status** — Eligible, Pending, Registered, Withdrew, or others as your candidacy moves forward

Select any row's **View** button to go to that Event's detail page.

If the list is empty, it means your teacher hasn't yet been invited to (or hasn't opened) an Event you're eligible for — nothing to do here until that changes.

### 5. Event / Candidate page

This is where the real work of registering happens — one page per Event Version you're a Candidate for. It's the most detailed page in the app, and it has its own in-app guided tour: select **Take a tour** at the top of the page anytime.

**Student Registration Period**: the dates under the Event's name. You can make changes only during this period, so finish everything — including recordings — before it ends.

**Candidate Requirements** (top card): a checklist of the basic information this specific Event needs from you before you can be registered — things like Birthday, Shirt Size, Height, Home Address, and Emergency Contact, depending on what this Event requires. Green means done and red means not done yet. Each one is a link straight to the Student Details or Emergency Contacts page where you can complete it.

**Registration**: shows your **voice part** and **program name** (how your name appears in printed programs) for this specific Event, plus your teacher's name. Select the pencil icon next to either field to edit it — your voice part is limited to the ones this Event offers to your grade, and your preferred voice part (from Student Details) is preselected automatically when it's one of the options. A **Withdraw from this Event** button is also here if you need to drop out — this takes effect immediately, you can't undo it yourself, and your teacher will be able to see that you've withdrawn.

**Application**: appears once this Event has published an application. Select **View** to read it (also available as a PDF download). If this Event uses electronic signatures ("EApplication"), you'll see two checkboxes inside the View window:

- **"I have signed"**
- **"My parent/guardian has reviewed and approved"**

Because there's no separate parent account in StudentFolder.info yet, you check both boxes yourself — the second one is you confirming, on your parent or guardian's behalf, that they've reviewed and approved it. If instead this Event uses a paper/PDF-only application, there's no self-signing step for you at all; your teacher certifies the paper copy once it's returned to them, and you'll simply see a "Certified" / "Not Certified" badge here.

The Application section is locked (View disabled, checkboxes unavailable) until every Candidate Requirement above is met — finish those first.

**Recordings**: appears only for Events that use remote (recorded) auditions. For each required recording slot:

- **Upload** a file if nothing's there yet (audio: mp3/wav/m4a, or video: mp4/mov, depending on what this Event asks for; up to 50MB).
- While a recording is **Pending Review**, you can **Replace** it or **Delete** it — deleting doesn't require you to have a replacement ready.
- Once your teacher **Approves** a recording, it locks — you can only listen to it, not delete or replace it. If it needs to change after approval, you'll need to ask your teacher.

**Pitch Files**: select the **Pitch Files** button near the top of the page to open reference audio/PDFs for your own voice part (plus anything marked for all voice parts). This is reference material only, and isn't part of any checklist. It's available from the start of the registration period until the Event's final deadline for teacher changes, which can be later than your own. If nothing shows up, either no files have been added yet for your voice part, or your teacher has chosen not to share pitch files with candidates for this Event.

**Payment**: if this Event takes electronic payment from students, and your specific teacher has opted in to it, you'll see **Pay Registration Fee** here while that fee is due. You can pay it from the start of the registration period until the final deadline for teacher changes or the start of adjudication, whichever comes first. If an Event also charges participation or housing fees, your teacher will tell you how they're paid. Your payment history is listed below, read-only. You may also see:

- **Payment being confirmed** — we're waiting for the payment service to confirm a payment you started, usually for a couple of minutes. Don't pay again. If you closed the payment page without paying, the button comes back after a while and you can try again.
- **Waived** — the Event has waived that fee for you, so there's nothing to pay.
- **Overpaid** — more has been paid than you owe. Ask your teacher about it.

If you don't see a Pay button at all, see Common questions.

**Three things can pause your ability to make changes on this page**, shown as a banner at the top:

- **Registration period** — before it opens and after it closes, the page is read-only. The banner gives the date and time.
- **Status lock** — once you're no longer in an active-registration status (for example, if you've withdrawn, been adjudicated, or the audition process has moved past registration for you), your voice part, program name, application signatures, recordings, and Withdraw button all become read-only. This is expected — those actions stop making sense once your candidacy has moved on.
- **Obligations pause** — if your teacher hasn't yet reviewed and accepted this Event's requirements with the Event organizer, registration is temporarily paused for every one of their students, including you. There's nothing to do on your end here — it resolves once your teacher takes action, and the banner disappears automatically once it does.

## Common questions

**My school isn't listed when I search — what do I do?**
Students can only join a school and teacher that are already set up in the system. Ask your teacher to complete their own registration on TheDirectorsRoom.com first (only teachers can add a new school).

**I picked my school but no teachers show up to select — why?**
That teacher hasn't finished verifying their school email yet. Check back later, or ask them directly to complete setup.

**Why can't I edit my voice part, sign my application, or change a recording anymore?**
Look at the banner at the top of the page. Most often the student registration period hasn't opened yet or has closed. It can also be that you've withdrawn or moved past registration, or that your teacher hasn't accepted the Event's requirements yet. If something needs to change after your registration period closes, ask your teacher right away; they may have a little more time than you do.

**I've finished everything, but I'm still "Pending" — why?**
Your teacher may still need to approve your recordings, certify your paper application, or refresh your status, so check with them. It can also be that the final deadline for registering has passed, or that your school has reached this Event's limit on how many students may audition.

**There's no parent account — how does my parent actually sign the application?**
There isn't a separate parent login. On an Event that uses electronic signatures, you check both boxes yourself — one for your own signature, and one confirming your parent/guardian has reviewed and approved it on your behalf.

**I don't see a Pay button for a fee I know is due — why?**
Electronic payment only appears when both (a) the Event has turned it on for students, and (b) your specific teacher has opted in to it. If either isn't true, that fee is likely being handled by your school instead — check with your teacher. The button also goes away once the fee is paid or waived, while a payment is being confirmed, and outside the dates when students can pay.

**I withdrew from an Event by mistake — can I undo it?**
Not from your own account — withdrawing is immediate and self-service, but there's no self-service "undo." Tell your teacher as soon as you can. They can't reverse a withdrawal you made yourself, but they can contact the Event's organizer for you.

**My recording upload got flagged — what does that mean?**
A flag just means the system noticed something about the file (for example, the file name mentions a different recording, or it's unusually short or long) and is asking your teacher to take a second look — it isn't a rejection, and you don't need to do anything unless your teacher asks you to re-upload.
