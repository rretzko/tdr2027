# Teacher Guide

*A guide to using TheDirectorsRoom.com as a music teacher/director.*

## 1. Welcome

TheDirectorsRoom.com is where you manage your students' participation in honors choir auditions — from building your class roster, to registering students for a given year's audition, to viewing their results once the audition is over.

A few terms are used precisely throughout the site and this guide, so it's worth knowing them up front:

- **Organization** — the group that sponsors the audition program (for example, a state music educators association).
- **Event** — the audition program itself, run once a year (or on whatever schedule the Organization sets).
- **Version** — one specific year's run of an Event. This is the thing you actually register students into — "the 2027 All-State Chorus," not "All-State Chorus" in general.
- **Ensemble** — a choir that comes out of a Version (Mixed Chorus, Treble Choir, etc.). Which Ensemble your students land in is decided after adjudication.
- **Voice Part** — S1, S2, A1, A2, T1, T2, B1, B2, and so on.
- **Student** — a person on your roster. A student's record follows them year to year.
- **Candidate** — one student's registration into one specific Version. The same student gets a new Candidate record every year they audition. A Candidate moves through a series of statuses as registration and adjudication proceed — more on that in the FAQ at the end of this guide.

Everything you do on the Teacher side of the site revolves around one loop: keep your roster current, register eligible students for a Version, get each one to "Registered," and then, after the audition, check their results.

## 2. Getting started

Create your account with your name, pronouns, email, cell phone, and a password, then select the link in the verification email. After that you can log in with either your email address or your cell phone number, or continue with Google or Facebook.

### The onboarding wizard

The first time you log in as a teacher, you'll be dropped into a five-step setup wizard before you can do anything else. You can't skip this — every other page requires it to be finished first. It only runs once; after you finish it, you go straight to your dashboard on future logins.

Your progress is saved after every step, so if you close the browser partway through, you'll pick back up where you left off.

**Step 1 — Link your school or studio.** Pick your state and start typing your school or studio's name. The wizard suggests real matches as you type (and lists nearby schools if you enter a zip code instead). If your school genuinely isn't in the system yet, choose "add a new school or studio" and enter its name, city, zip code, and county yourself.

**Step 2 — Your role and subjects.** Tell the system whether you're the primary teacher/director or a co-teacher/assistant director at that school. If you're taking over for someone who left, check "I'm replacing a teacher who is no longer at this school" and pick that teacher from the list. Choose "Someone not listed" if you need to type the name. Then check every subject you teach there (chorus, band, orchestra, etc.) — this drives what shows up later when you add students.

**Step 3 — Build your roster.** If any students are already on file at your school, you'll see them listed with a checkbox — check the ones who are yours and assign them a subject. Below that, add any brand-new students by typing in name, grade, and subject; click "+ Add a student" for each additional row. You can leave this step mostly empty and add students later from the Students page if you'd rather not do it all now.

**Step 4 — Sponsoring organizations.** Optional. If you belong to any of the organizations listed (shown in a nested tree — parent organizations with their sub-organizations underneath), select them here.

**Step 5 — You're all set.** A short summary screen, then "Go to my dashboard" finishes onboarding for good.

### Verify your school email

**This step is easy to miss.** Students, Registrations, and Results stay closed until your email at an active school is verified.

1. Select **Schools** in the sidebar and edit your school.
2. Enter your **School email** and save. It must be on your school's own domain, not a personal provider such as Gmail.
3. Open the verification email and select the link within three days. If your school email is the same address you log in with and you've already verified it, there's nothing more to do.

If you said you're replacing a teacher, that teacher's current students at the school move to your roster once your link to the school is verified. Candidates move too, with two exceptions: a Version you haven't been invited to, and a candidate with a payment made by a teacher. Those stay with the previous teacher, so contact the Event Manager about them.

**Once a year, everyone verifies again.** Verifications are reset so that teachers who have left a school drop off its lists. You'll get a new email; select its link and your school becomes active again, with your roster as you left it.

### Finding your way around

- **Sidebar** — Schools, Students, Organizations, Registrations, and Results. Registrations and Results appear once you have an invitation, an active candidate, or a Version you can ask to join. Events appears only if you hold a role such as judge or Event Manager.
- **Fast Pass** — at the top of the sidebar, a menu of the pages you've visited most recently and most often.
- **Organizations** — select the organizations you belong to, and add your membership number, expiration date, and membership card image. Some Versions need the card for the Estimate Form (§10).
- **Profile** — your name, photo, and contact details. Profile also leads to Email Preferences (§11).
- **Feedback** — report a bug, ask for an improvement, or send a comment. The History tab shows earlier reports.
- **User Guides** — this guide, the Student Guide, and the Adjudicator Guide.

Many pages have a **Take a tour** button that walks you through what's on screen.

## 3. Your student roster

**Page: Students**

This is your list of students — add them here once, and they carry forward every year. It shows your students at every school where you're active and verified. Search by name, and use the filters that apply to you: All/Current/Alumni, subject, and school. A student you teach in more than one subject appears once, with the subjects listed together.

**Adding a student.** Select **Add student** and choose the school, grade, and subject(s). Name, pronouns, and shirt size are required; email, cell phone, birthday, height, instrument, voice part, home address, and emergency contacts are optional here, though a Version may require some of them.

**Watch for matches.** As you type, the site looks for the same student already on file, and asks you to resolve close matches before you save. This prevents one student from ending up with two disconnected records.

- **Already on your roster** — select **Open existing record**. Choose **Add as a new student** only if it really is a different person.
- **At your own school** — select **This is my student** and pick the subject(s). They join your roster right away.
- **No active teacher anywhere** — select **This is my student**. They join your roster right away, with no approval needed.
- **Another teacher's student** — select **Request to add**. That student's current teacher(s) get an approval email that's good for seven days. The student shows as **Pending** on your roster until it's approved, and is removed from your roster if it's denied. To withdraw your request, choose **Cancel request**.
- **Someone else entirely** — select **Not this student** and carry on.

**Signed up, but haven't joined a school.** Sometimes a student creates a StudentFolder.info account with your school's email domain but never finishes joining a school, often because the school's mail filter blocked our verification message. Those students are listed at the top of this page. Select **Add to my roster** instead of creating a new record, or **Not my student** to clear the name.

If your school is a studio rather than a school, you'll also be asked for the student's actual home school, since a studio doesn't track grade levels on its own.

**Editing a student.** Select **Edit** (the pencil) to open the same form pre-filled, plus a few extra tools: upload or remove a profile photo, and reset the student's password (it resets to their email address, lowercased). Note: if your request to add a student is still pending approval, you can't edit that student's profile yet.

**Deactivate vs. Remove.** Deactivate marks a student Inactive and stops enrolling them in new Versions, without deleting anything — use this for a student who's left your program but whose history you want to keep. To undo it, choose **Reactivate**; the student is enrolled in any active Version you're invited to that they qualify for. A student who has since joined another school can't be reactivated. Remove takes the student off your roster in every subject you teach them at that school, and can't be undone. If the student isn't on anyone else's roster and their account was never used, the record itself is deleted too.

**Sharing your roster with a co-teacher.** If another teacher at your school needs access to the same students (say, an assistant director), that's granted from the **Schools** page, not here — open the "Co-Teachers" action on your school's row to grant (or revoke) another teacher at that school access to your roster. Both of you must be active and verified there. It's one-directional: granting access to someone doesn't automatically give you access to theirs, and only the teacher who granted access can revoke it. Once granted, your shared students' Candidates show up for your co-teacher, as well as you, on the Version Dashboard (see §7).

## 4. Finding an open Version — the Registrations page

**Page: Registrations**

This is your entry point into any actual audition cycle. Every Version you have some standing in is sorted into one of three groups — a Version that doesn't fit any of them simply isn't shown:

- **Open for Registration** — Versions where the registration window is currently open and you've already been invited. Click **Manage** to go straight to that Version's dashboard.
- **Invitation Available** — Versions where the window is open and you're eligible, but you haven't been invited yet. Click **Request Invitation** to ask (see §5).
- **Active Candidates** — Versions where the registration window has since closed, but you still have students enrolled there. Useful for keeping an eye on something after the fact.

Each card shows the Event name, the next upcoming milestone date, how many candidates you have there, and — once a Version has a published Obligations document — a colored badge (green Accepted / amber Awaiting / red Rejected) linking straight to that document.

If nothing shows up at all, it means no Event Manager has opened a Version you're eligible for yet.

## 5. Requesting an invitation

**Page: Request Invitation** (reached via "Invitation Available" on the Registrations page)

Being *eligible* for a Version (based on the county of a school where you're active and verified) doesn't automatically mean you're *invited* — the Event Manager controls the invitation list. If you're eligible but not yet invited, this page lets you ask. Click **Request Invitation**; the button becomes "Request Sent" and disables itself while the request is pending. The Event Manager gets an email with your name, school, county, and membership info, and can approve or deny it from that email. Approval enrolls all your eligible students at once.

If a request is denied, you're not locked out — the page shows a note that you're welcome to request again, and the button re-enables.

## 6. Version Obligations — read this carefully

**Page: Obligations** (reached from a Version card's Obligations badge, or a redirect the first time you try to open a Version you're invited to)

Many Versions have a "Teacher Obligations" document — rules and commitments the Event Manager wants every participating teacher to agree to before registering students. If one exists and is published, you must **Accept** it before you can do anything else in that Version: no dashboard, no candidate pages, no Pitch Files, no Estimate Form. The very first time you try to open an invited Version, you'll be sent here automatically if you haven't responded yet.

**This is an iron gate, and it goes both ways.**

- If you **Accept**, a **Review Eligible Students** button appears. Select it to open that Version's dashboard.
- If you **Reject**, your participation in that Version stops immediately — every candidate you already had enrolled there is withdrawn on the spot, and you cannot enroll anyone new until you accept again. The Version's registration pages become unreachable until you do.
- If you later change your mind and **Accept again**, access is restored and the candidates that the rejection had withdrawn are automatically brought back to wherever they would have naturally been (so a student who was fully checklisted before the rejection lands right back on "Registered," not back at square one).

Two limits worth knowing. Accepting brings back every candidate with a Teacher Withdrawn status, including any you withdrew yourself for another reason, so check your roster afterward. And after the deadline for teacher changes (§8), a candidate who is brought back can't become Registered again.

You can return at any time from the Obligations badge on the Registrations page to review what you agreed to, change your answer, or select **Download PDF**.

## 7. The Version Dashboard — your main workspace

**Page: a Version's dashboard** (click "Manage" or "View" from the Registrations page)

This is where you'll spend most of your time during a registration window. It has an in-app guided tour — look for the "Take a tour" button near the top — which walks through everything below interactively; it launches automatically the first time you land here and can be replayed any time.

**Upcoming Deadlines and Registration Summary.** At the top, a timeline of the next few milestone dates for this Version, and two quick summary tables — your Registered candidates by voice part, and everyone by status (Eligible, Pending, Registered) — both reflecting your whole roster regardless of any search/filter below.

**My Candidates.** Your actual working roster for this Version — every student you (or a co-teacher who's shared their roster with you) have enrolled. Once you're invited, students become Candidates here automatically if they're active on your roster, active at your school, and in a grade the Version accepts; there's no manual "enroll a student" step to remember. Search by name, and filter by voice part, status, or school.

Each candidate row shows the student's name and email, voice part, and:

- **Checklist** — a strip of green/amber/red pills, one per requirement this Version collects (Program name, Voice part valid for grade, Emergency contact, Home address, signatures, recordings, and so on). Green means done, amber means partially done, red means not started. This is designed to answer "why isn't this student Registered yet?" at a glance — whatever's still red or amber is what's blocking them.
- **Waiver badge** — shown when the event has waived a fee for this student (§8).
- **Status** — Eligible, Pending, Registered, or Withdrawn (see the FAQ for the full list).
- **Paid** — what's been credited toward that candidate so far, less any refunds.
- **Manage** — opens the full Candidate Detail page (§8).
- **Refresh** — re-checks the checklist against current data (useful right after you've made a change elsewhere that should unlock the next status).
- **Withdraw** — pulls the student out of this Version. It's available while the student is Eligible, Pending, or Registered; you'll be asked to confirm.
- **Reinstate** — replaces Withdraw on a student you withdrew. The status resets to Eligible and the checklist is rechecked right away, so a complete checklist goes straight back to Registered. After the deadline for teacher changes, Reinstate can only bring a student back to Pending or Eligible. You can't reinstate a student who withdrew on StudentFolder.info; contact the Event Manager.

**Quick links.** Pitch Files, Estimate Form, Group Payment, and Payment Register are one click away from the top of the roster (Pitch Files is covered in §9, Estimate Form in §10; Payment Register is a downloadable PDF/CSV of every payment recorded for your roster in this Version).

**Letting families pay online.** If student e-payment is turned on for this Version, check **Enable e-payment for all of your candidates in this Version** to let your students pay on StudentFolder.info. It applies to your whole roster and doesn't affect group payment.

**Group payment.** If teacher e-payment is turned on, check off the candidates in the roster, then choose **Pay Registration Fee for Selected**. Once the Version closes, the choices become Participation and Housing, for Accepted students. Anyone who has already paid in full has to be deselected first, and a waived fee is never charged.

Group payment takes two steps, and **the second one is yours**:

1. Pay. The payment appears under **Pending Payments**, usually for a couple of minutes, while the payment service confirms it. Don't pay again. If you closed the payment page without paying, the site tells you when you can try again.
2. Once confirmed, it moves to **Your Unreconciled Payments**. Select **Allocate** and enter an amount for each student. Until you do, the payment doesn't count toward anyone's balance.

Some Versions accept online payment only. A notice here and on the Estimate Form tells you so; please don't mail a check for those.

**Sharing candidates with a co-teacher.** If you share roster access with another teacher at the same school, in either direction (§3), a panel here says which way access runs (one-way or both) and lets either of you decide whose name the shared candidates should be recorded under for this Version — useful for keeping one consistent point of contact for correspondence and payment records.

## 8. Managing one candidate — the Candidate Detail page

**Page: Candidate Detail** (click Manage on any roster row)

This is the record for one student's registration into one Version. It's organized as a set of cards, each with its own Edit button that opens a small form — nothing is left in an always-open edit state, so the page stays readable even once everything's filled in.

- **Registration Checklist** — the same checklist from the dashboard row, shown in full here.
- **Student** — name, and this Candidate's voice part (not the student's general-profile voice part — you can set a different one specifically for this audition), birthday, and height. Only voice parts offered to the student's grade can be chosen. If the current one isn't offered, you'll see a warning; choose the part that matches the recordings.
- **Home Address** — only shown if this Version requires it.
- **Emergency Contacts** — add or edit any number of contacts; which fields are required (cell, email) depends on this Version's settings.
- **Program Name** — how the student's name should appear in the printed program. Defaults to their real name but is freely editable.
- **Application** — click View to see the actual merged application document (the real legal form students/parents/teachers sign), along with the sign-off checkboxes. Two modes exist:
  - **PDF mode** — signatures happen on paper. You check one box, certifying that the student, parent/guardian, teacher, and principal signatures are present, complete, and have integrity, once you've reviewed the physical form.
  - **eApplication mode** — signatures happen digitally. If your student has their own StudentFolder.info account, they and their parent sign it themselves there. If a student hasn't set up their own account yet, you can still check "Candidate signed" / "Parent signed" on their behalf as a stopgap — whichever of you (teacher or student) acts last is what counts.
- **Recordings** — for a remote audition, upload each required audio or video file here, or review what the student uploaded. Every file needs your **Approve**, including ones you upload yourself. Audio can be MP3, WAV, or M4A; video can be MP4 or MOV; the limit is 50 MB per file.
  - **Approve** makes the file available to the judges. Students can no longer change or remove it.
  - **Replace** swaps in a new file, even for an approved recording. The old file is deleted and the new one needs approval again, so a Registered candidate drops to Pending until you approve it.
  - **Reject** (or **Remove**, once approved) deletes the file outright and re-opens that slot.
  - **Review Suggested** is a flag for a possible misfile, such as the wrong slot or an unusual length. It never rejects anything on your behalf.
- **Payment** — if teacher e-payment is on, **Pay Registration Fee** (or, once the Version closes, Participation or Housing) sends you to a secure checkout for this one student. While the payment service confirms it, the button is replaced by "payment being confirmed"; there's no need to pay again. A fee waived by the event is marked **Waived**, with nothing to pay, and an overpayment is shown too. Use **Record Payment** for a registration fee you collected by check, purchase order, cash, or another way, or for a refund; it doesn't charge anyone. Payment history is listed below.

Use **Refresh Status** any time you want the checklist and status re-evaluated immediately rather than waiting for your next visit.

### When registration is held or closed

**The deadline for teacher changes.** This is the Version's **Final Teacher Changes** date or, if there isn't one, its **Postmark Deadline**. Watch for it under Upcoming Deadlines. After it passes:

- Eligible and Pending candidates can't become Registered, even if the checklist is completed. The candidate's page says so in a yellow notice.
- The voice part is read-only.
- Recordings can't be uploaded, replaced, approved, or removed.

Students who are already Registered stay Registered, but be careful: if you undo a required item, such as a signature certification, the student drops to Pending and **can't come back**. Contact the event coordinators for any exception.

**Audition cap reached.** Some Versions limit how many students each school may audition. A student who is complete but over the cap stays Pending, with a notice on this page. Contact the event coordinators if an exception is in order.

## 9. Pitch Files

**Page: Pitch Files** (linked from the Version Dashboard's quick links)

A read-only library of reference audio/video/PDF material for this Version's audition — scales, solo excerpts, quintet mixes, and the like — organized by voice part. Filter by voice part or by file type. A file marked for "ALL" voice parts always shows regardless of which voice part you've filtered to, since it applies across the board.

Note that some Versions restrict pitch files to candidates only (visible on StudentFolder.info, not here) — if that's the case for a given Version, this page will tell you plainly rather than showing an empty list with no explanation.

## 10. Estimate Form

**Page: Estimate Form** (linked from the Version Dashboard's quick links)

A printable invoice summarizing what you owe (or have overpaid) for your registered candidates in this Version — meant to be downloaded and, where required, physically mailed in with your paperwork. A fee the event has waived shows as Waived and isn't included in what you owe. If you have registered candidates at only one school, you'll see the summary right on the page — candidate count, fee subtotal, electronic payments received, and balance due — with a Download PDF button. If you have candidates at more than one school, you'll instead see a list of schools, each with its own separate Download PDF link, since fees, mailing addresses, and (if required) membership card documentation are all resolved per school.

If this Version requires a membership card, that section shows either your card on file or a note that one hasn't been uploaded yet, with a link to add it under Organizations.

## 11. The Director Weekly Update email

Every Wednesday morning until adjudication begins, you'll get an email for each active Version you've been invited to, unless you've rejected its Obligations. It lists the Version's dates, your students by status, fees due and paid, your Registered students, and, most usefully, **what each Pending student is still missing**.

To stop it, use the unsubscribe link in the email, or turn it off under **Profile → Email Preferences**, where you can turn it back on again. Account emails, such as verification links, approvals, and payment notices, are always sent.

## 12. Results

**Pages: Results, and the version-specific Results page reached from it**

Once the Event Manager closes a Version and releases results, it shows up on this list with a **Results** button. The Event Manager may also choose to email you when they're ready. Until that happens, a Version simply doesn't appear here — there's no way to see partial or in-progress results early.

On a released Version's page you'll see every one of your candidates who reached a final audition outcome: **Accepted** (with the Ensemble they were placed into), **Not Accepted**, **No Show**, or **Incomplete**, along with their score if one was recorded. On a computer, select a column heading to sort by name, voice part, score, result, or ensemble. If your candidates are at more than one school, a school filter appears, and if you have more than one released Version, a menu lets you switch between them.

When scores were recorded, you can view or download a score report for each school (one page per candidate) and for each individual candidate. Each page is headed by the student's name and voice part, so treat it as private. If the Event Manager enabled "Share Results," a **Full Score Report** for the whole audition is available too; it may show "Preparing…" for a short time after results are released.

Participation and housing fees, where a Version charges them, become payable for Accepted students once the Version closes. If teacher e-payment is on, pay them from that student's Candidate Detail page or by group payment (§7).

## 13. Common questions

**Why is a student's status stuck at "Pending"?** One or more registration requirements aren't complete yet. Open that candidate's Manage page and look at the Registration Checklist — anything not green is what's holding them back. Fix it, then click Refresh Status if it doesn't update on its own. If everything is green, look for a notice about the deadline for teacher changes or the school's audition cap (§8).

**What are all the possible Candidate statuses?** In order: **Eligible** (qualifies for this Version, nothing done yet) → **Pending** (some requirements met, not all) → **Registered** (fully checklisted). A candidate can be withdrawn by you (**Teacher Withdrawn**) or by the student (**Withdrew**), or, once Registered, move on to adjudication and land on **Accepted**, **Not Accepted**, **No Show**, or **Incomplete**. A candidate accepted into an Ensemble can later be marked **Declined** (they turned down the spot) or **Removed** (didn't meet rehearsal requirements) by the people managing that later stage.

**Why did all my students suddenly get withdrawn from a Version?** Most likely you (or someone with access to your obligations response) rejected that Version's Teacher Obligations document. See §6 — this is by design, and re-accepting brings everyone back.

**Can I enroll a new student manually?** No — there's no manual "enroll" button. Enrollment happens automatically the moment a student becomes eligible: when you add them to your roster, when you're invited to a Version they qualify for, or when that Version becomes Active. Add the student on the Students page (or claim/accept an invitation), and they'll appear on the Version Dashboard on their own.

**A recording I approved needs to be replaced — how?** Select **Replace** and upload the new file, then approve it. This must be done before the deadline for teacher changes (§8).

**I paid for a group, but my students still show $0.00. Why?** The payment is either still being confirmed or hasn't been allocated. Look for it under Pending Payments or Your Unreconciled Payments (§7).

**The site sends me to Schools when I try to open Students. Why?** You don't have an active school with a verified school email. Verify it as described in §2; this also happens after the yearly reset.

**Why can't I see results for a Version I know already happened?** Results aren't visible to teachers until the Event Manager explicitly releases them (a separate step from simply closing the Version for other administrative reasons). If a Version you expect to see results for isn't listed on the Results page, they haven't been released yet — check back later.

**A student I want to add already looks like they're already in the system under another teacher — what happens?** If they're currently claimed by another active teacher, you'll be offered **Request to add** — that teacher gets an approval email, and the student is added to your roster once they approve. If the student has no active teacher, you get access immediately.

**Do I need to do anything special for a co-taught roster?** Only if you want to share access. Grant your co-teacher access from the Schools page; once granted, your students show up under "My Candidates" on your co-teacher's Version Dashboard. You see theirs only if they grant you access in return. Either of you can decide whose name a shared Version's candidates are recorded under.
